# Responsible AI Principles

## Purpose

Bharat AI Commons aims to support the responsible discovery, documentation, development, and evaluation of AI resources for Indian languages and Indian contexts.

Responsible AI is not a separate activity from building AI infrastructure. It should inform how resources are sourced, models are described, evaluations are conducted, and findings are communicated.

These principles are informed by established approaches to AI risk management, including the [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Transparency and Traceability

- Document the source and provenance of datasets, models, and other resources.
- Record licences, usage restrictions, and relevant attribution requirements.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and unverified claims.
- Make evaluation methods and limitations understandable to readers.

### 2. Privacy and Data Protection

- Respect applicable privacy and data protection requirements.
- Do not publish personal, confidential, or sensitive information without appropriate authorisation.
- Consider consent, data minimisation, and potential re-identification risks when working with datasets.
- Do not assume publicly accessible data is unrestricted for reuse.

### 3. Fairness and Language Representation

- Recognise the diversity of India's languages, scripts, dialects, and communities.
- Avoid generalising results from one language or population to others without evidence.
- Document known coverage gaps and potential biases.
- Encourage evaluation across relevant language varieties and use cases.

### 4. Evaluation and Reproducibility

- Use clearly defined tasks, datasets, metrics, and evaluation procedures.
- Record model versions, configurations, dependencies, and relevant experimental conditions.
- Distinguish developer-reported results from independently reproduced findings.
- Report limitations and uncertainty alongside performance results.

### 5. Safety and Appropriate Use

- Consider foreseeable misuse and potential harm associated with datasets, models, and tools.
- Document known limitations and relevant safety considerations.
- Apply additional scrutiny to resources intended for high-impact contexts, such as healthcare, education, and public services.
- Avoid presenting experimental resources as validated for consequential real-world decisions without appropriate evidence.

### 6. Licensing and Intellectual Property

- Verify applicable licences and terms before recommending reuse.
- Preserve attribution and other licence obligations.
- Do not assume that a model, dataset, or tool is open source merely because it is free to access.
- Respect third-party intellectual property and contribution rights.

### 7. Accountability and Community Participation

- Document who maintains a resource where that information is available.
- Encourage constructive review and transparent correction of errors.
- Provide a way to raise concerns about inaccurate documentation or potentially harmful content.
- Welcome perspectives from researchers, developers, students, language communities, and other relevant stakeholders.

## Applying These Principles

These principles should inform the development of:

- Dataset and model catalogues
- Documentation templates
- Benchmark and evaluation methodologies
- Reusable tools and scripts
- Research collaborations and community projects

The level of review should be proportionate to the resource, intended use, and potential consequences.

## Current Status

This document establishes initial guiding principles for Bharat AI Commons. It is not a certification, formal compliance framework, or guarantee that every listed resource has undergone a responsible AI assessment.

The principles may evolve through research, practical experience, and community feedback.
