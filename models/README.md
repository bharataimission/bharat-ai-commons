# Open Model Catalogue

## Purpose

The Open Model Catalogue documents openly available AI models that may be useful for Indian languages, Indian contexts, and India's open AI ecosystem.

Our goal is to help students, researchers, developers, universities, and organisations discover relevant models and understand their capabilities, limitations, access conditions, and suitability for different use cases.

## What We Aim to Document

The catalogue will progressively cover:

- **Indian-language models:** Models supporting one or more Indian languages.
- **Multilingual models:** Models that include Indian languages in their supported language set.
- **Speech models:** Automatic speech recognition, text-to-speech, and related speech technologies.
- **Translation models:** Models for translation between Indian languages and between Indian and other languages.
- **General-purpose models:** Open models that may be useful for Indian applications.
- **Domain-specific models:** Models relevant to areas such as education, healthcare, agriculture, and public services, where appropriate.

## Model Documentation Standards

Each model entry should capture, where available:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Model name | Official name of the model |
| Developer | Organisation, research group, or community |
| Model link | Official model page or repository |
| Supported languages | Languages explicitly documented by the developer |
| Intended use | Primary capabilities and use cases |
| Model architecture or family | Information published by the developer |
| Model weights availability | Whether weights are publicly accessible |
| Licence | Exact licence and any relevant restrictions |
| Access requirements | Download, API, registration, or other requirements |
| Evaluation evidence | Published benchmarks or reproducible evaluation results |
| Known limitations | Documented limitations, risks, or gaps |
| Last reviewed | Date the information was last checked |

Unknown or unverified information should be marked clearly rather than assumed.

## Inclusion Principles

Models documented in this catalogue should:

1. Have a publicly accessible official reference or credible technical source.
2. Include traceable information about their developer and intended use.
3. Have their licence and access conditions recorded accurately where available.
4. Clearly distinguish developer-reported claims from independently evaluated results.
5. Include relevant limitations and evaluation gaps where known.

Inclusion in this catalogue does not imply endorsement by Bharat AI Mission.

## Responsible Documentation

- Do not describe a model as open source solely because it is free to use.
- Record the actual licence and access conditions.
- Do not claim that a model supports a language without supporting evidence.
- Do not present benchmark results without their source and evaluation context.
- Respect applicable licences, attribution requirements, privacy, and responsible AI practices.

## Current Status

This catalogue is being established. Model entries will be added as information is identified, reviewed, and documented.

The initial priority is to establish a reliable, transparent catalogue before expanding into comparative evaluation.

## How to Contribute

During this initial phase, the catalogue is being prepared for structured community review. Please read the repository's [README](../README.md) and [contribution guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md) before proposing changes.

Future contributions may include model discovery, documentation, source verification, language coverage analysis, and evaluation.
