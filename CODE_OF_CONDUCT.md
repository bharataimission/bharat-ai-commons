# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Bharat AI Commons is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project, regardless of background, experience, language, nationality, gender, disability, or personal identity.

We aim to build an inclusive open-source community that advances AI resources for Indian languages and Indian contexts.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback from beginners and experienced contributors alike.
- Critique ideas and technical work without personal attacks.
- Respect different linguistic, cultural, and academic perspectives.
- Provide accurate information and acknowledge sources and contributions.
- Respect licences, privacy, intellectual property, and community boundaries.
- Help maintain a collaborative environment focused on learning and shared progress.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without consent, and other conduct that undermines a safe and constructive community.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns about conduct should be reported privately to the project maintainers through an appropriate contact channel published by Bharat AI Mission.

Please include relevant context and evidence where possible. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practicable, with due consideration for everyone involved.

A public issue should not be used to disclose private information or sensitive details about a complaint.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Bharat AI Commons project spaces and to other settings where an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate responses. Actions may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces in serious cases.

Responses should be proportionate, fair, and focused on protecting the community.

## Continuous Improvement

This document may evolve as the project and its community grow.
