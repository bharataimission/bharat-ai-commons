# 🇮🇳 Bharat AI Commons

### Building shared foundations for open AI in India.

**Bharat AI Commons** is a community-led initiative by Bharat AI Mission to make AI resources for Indian languages and Indian contexts easier to discover, understand, evaluate, and contribute to.

Our belief is simple: democratizing AI requires more than models. It requires accessible resources, transparent documentation, meaningful evaluation, and collaboration across developers, researchers, academic institutions, and language communities.

> Our mission: Build an open, discoverable, and responsibly maintained commons of AI resources for India's diverse languages and communities.

## 🎯 What We Are Building

### 1. Dataset Directory

A structured catalogue of existing datasets relevant to Indian languages and contexts.

We aim to document:

- Languages, scripts, and supported tasks
- Dataset sources and access instructions
- Licensing and attribution requirements
- Known limitations and intended use

**Explore:** [Dataset Catalogue](datasets/catalog.csv) · [Dataset Documentation Template](docs/dataset-documentation-template.md)

### 2. Documentation Standards

Reusable templates that help contributors describe datasets, models, and other resources consistently, including provenance, collection methods, intended use, limitations, licensing, and ethical considerations.

**Explore:** [Dataset Template](docs/dataset-documentation-template.md) · [Model Template](docs/model-documentation-template.md)

### 3. Evaluation and Benchmarks

A pathway for discovering existing benchmarks and exploring transparent, reproducible ways to evaluate AI systems across Indian-language tasks.

**Explore:** [Benchmarks and Evaluation](benchmarks/README.md)

### 4. Language and Resource Gap Mapping

A community-maintained view of available resources and gaps across Indian languages, scripts, text, speech, and other AI applications.

**Explore:** [Indian Language Resources](language-resources/README.md)

### 5. Open Model Catalogue

A structured starting point for documenting models, their language coverage, capabilities, access conditions, licences, and evaluation evidence.

**Explore:** [Model Catalogue](models/catalog.csv) · [Model Catalogue Guidelines](models/README.md)

### 6. Tools and Utilities

A foundation for reusable scripts, dataset validation utilities, language-processing tools, and reproducible evaluation workflows.

**Explore:** [Tools and Utilities](tools/README.md)

## 🚀 Our First Milestone: Discover and Document

We are starting with a practical first step: **cataloguing and documenting existing resources before attempting to create new datasets.**

Our initial priorities are:

- Discover credible, publicly documented Indian-language AI resources.
- Record source, language, task, licence, and access details.
- Identify gaps and opportunities for community collaboration.
- Establish consistent documentation and review practices.
- Invite researchers, students, developers, and language experts to contribute.

This is an evolving initiative. Initial catalogue entries require review, and resource availability, licensing, and usage restrictions must be verified before use.

We are not yet claiming a comprehensive dataset collection, an independently validated benchmark suite, or an endorsed model leaderboard.

## 🤝 Who Can Contribute?

Everyone interested in open AI is welcome, including:

- Students and first-time open-source contributors
- AI/ML developers and data scientists
- Researchers and academic institutions
- Indian-language experts and translators
- Dataset creators and evaluators
- Responsible AI practitioners

You do not need to be an AI expert to contribute. Documentation, resource discovery, link verification, and language expertise are valuable contributions.

## 🌱 Guiding Principles

- **Open collaboration:** Build together and share knowledge.
- **Transparency:** Document sources, licences, methods, and limitations.
- **Inclusion:** Recognize India's linguistic and cultural diversity.
- **Responsible use:** Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and applicable restrictions.
- **Quality over quantity:** Prefer trustworthy, well-documented resources over unverified lists.
- **Reproducibility:** Make evaluation methods and results understandable and repeatable.
- **Community ownership:** Encourage contributions from diverse participants.

For more detail, see our [Responsible AI Principles](responsible-ai/README.md).

## 🧭 Relationship with Bharat AI Mission

Bharat AI Commons is part of **Bharat AI Mission**, an open-source community working toward the democratization of AI.

The Commons focuses on shared resources, documentation, and evaluation. Complementary initiatives such as **AI Builders Lab** focus on learning, experimentation, and building practical AI applications.

Together, these initiatives aim to connect discovery, learning, research, and practical innovation across India's open AI ecosystem.

## 📄 Licensing and Attribution

The repository's MIT licence applies to eligible code and original materials covered by that licence. Individual datasets, models, and third-party resources may have separate terms.

Contributors must document the applicable source, licence, attribution, and access restrictions for each entry. Inclusion in a catalogue does not imply endorsement or unrestricted permission to reuse a resource.

See the repository's [MIT Licence](LICENSE) for details.
