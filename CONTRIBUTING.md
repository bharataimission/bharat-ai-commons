# Contributing to Bharat AI Commons

Thank you for your interest in Bharat AI Commons.

Bharat AI Commons is an initiative under Bharat AI Mission to strengthen the shared foundations of India's open AI ecosystem, beginning with Indian-language AI resources and evaluation.

Our goal is to make existing resources easier to discover, understand, document, and evaluate.

## Current Project Stage

**Status: Initial setup and resource review.**

We are establishing the repository structure, documentation standards, and resource review process before formally opening the project for broader community contributions.

Please check the repository README for current priorities and announcements before starting work.

## Areas of Focus

Our planned areas of work include:

- **Datasets:** Discover and document existing AI datasets relevant to Indian languages and contexts.
- **Models:** Catalogue models, supported languages, capabilities, access conditions, and limitations.
- **Benchmarks:** Document evaluation resources, methodologies, metrics, and reproducibility requirements.
- **Language resources:** Identify linguistic resources, text-processing tools, and language coverage gaps.
- **Tools:** Develop reusable utilities and reference implementations.
- **Responsible AI:** Improve documentation of provenance, licensing, privacy, limitations, and responsible use.

## Contribution Principles

We aim to maintain a repository that is:

- Transparent about sources, methods, and limitations.
- Respectful of intellectual property, licensing, privacy, and consent.
- Inclusive of India's linguistic and cultural diversity.
- Focused on quality, accuracy, and reproducibility.
- Welcoming to students, researchers, developers, and language experts.

## Resource Documentation Requirements

When documenting an existing dataset, model, benchmark, or tool, provide as much of the following information as can be verified:

- Resource name and official source.
- Creator or maintaining organization.
- Supported languages, tasks, and modalities.
- Access instructions and applicable license.
- Attribution and redistribution requirements.
- Known limitations and relevant technical documentation.

Mark unknown information as unknown or not documented. Do not guess or present unverified information as fact.

## Data and Licensing

Do not upload or redistribute third-party datasets, model weights, private data, or other materials unless the applicable terms explicitly permit it.

Prefer linking to official sources and documenting the relevant access and licensing conditions.

Do not include personal information, confidential information, credentials, or sensitive data in the repository.

## Review and Quality

Proposed changes should be focused, clearly explained, and supported by reliable sources where applicable.

Technical contributions should include appropriate tests or reproducible instructions when relevant.

Resource listings and community reviews do not automatically imply endorsement, certification, or independent scientific validation by Bharat AI Mission.

## How to Participate

While the project is in its initial setup phase, you can explore the repository and follow its development.

Formal contribution instructions, prioritized issues, and review expectations will be published when the repository is ready to open for broader participation.

## Community Standards

All participants are expected to follow the repository's Code of Conduct and communicate respectfully.

## Maintainers

Bharat AI Commons is maintained under Bharat AI Mission. Project scope, review processes, and contribution priorities may evolve as the initiative develops.

Thank you for supporting open AI resources for India.
